TCGTracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

TCGTracker ("the app") is a life and counter tracker for tabletop card games, published by AzureCardCafe ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and who handles it.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own.
  • We do not collect, receive, or sell your personal information.
  • Your matches and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

To keep your games and preferences between sessions, the app saves these on your device only:

  • the game you selected and the active match (life totals and counters);
  • player panel colors and the shared text color;
  • default starting values for each game and the Commander setting;
  • whether you own the ad-free upgrade.

This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

Unless you buy the ad-free upgrade, the app shows one small banner ad during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure, and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information.

Google handles this information under its own policies. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings. On Android, open Settings and look for Google, then Ads. On iPhone and iPad, the app does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

The ad-free upgrade

The one-time ad-free purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details. The store tells the app that the purchase succeeded, and the app saves that on your device so ads stay hidden, even offline. Your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only to load ads and to check purchases with the app store. Tracking a game works without an internet connection.

Children

The app is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Google and Apple handle information as described above for ads and purchases.

Your choices

  • Delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it or clearing its data.
  • Stop seeing ads by buying the ad-free upgrade.
  • Reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
